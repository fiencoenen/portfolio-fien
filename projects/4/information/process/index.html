A moodboard exploring the reality of being a newcomer in Breda, highlighting the contrast between the lively student city image and the loneliness and social isolation some students experience.

An initial booklet concept intended to offer comfort and recognition to students, using a visual contrast between the expectations and reality of student life in Breda

The concept evolved into a manifesto, acting as a call to action for Breda's decision-makers, highlighting student loneliness and urging greater attention to social connection and a sense of belonging

Photographs taken by me of student rooms and public spaces with untapped potential for social connection, capturing the reality of student life in Breda

Qoutes from students sharing their experiences of living in Breda, adding a personal, diary-like feel that gives voice to their individual stories
