Fien Coenen (b. 2006), a Communication & Multimedia Design student with a love for graphic design, craftwork and visual storytelling.

I’ve always enjoyed making things and finding different ways to bring an idea to life. I like to work from subjects that interest me personally, especially when they have something to say. I’m drawn to work that can make people look at something a little differently, whether that is through a playful concept or a strong visual.

My work moves between digital and analogue ways of making. I enjoy experimenting with different techniques and discovering new ways to express an idea. I’m always curious to see where an idea can take me and how i can develop it further through making.
